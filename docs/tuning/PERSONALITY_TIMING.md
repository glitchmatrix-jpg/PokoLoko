# PokoLoko — Personality Timing

Poko responds sooner, moves a little faster, glances more often, settles after drag more quickly, and uses shorter sleep/activity phrases. Loko is deliberate rather than sluggish: longer attentive holds, slower turns, longer reading/laptop sessions, and longer wake recovery.

Calm extends idle space and suppresses salient activity. Balanced is the authored default. Lively creates more opportunities without bypassing legal transitions or interruption safety.

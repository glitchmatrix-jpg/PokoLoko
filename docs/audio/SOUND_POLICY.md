# PokoLoko — Sound Policy

Step 23 provides a safe audio runtime but intentionally ships no placeholder sound effects. No original or licensed PokoLoko sound pack was supplied; generic beeps would lower the product bar and become irritating.

Future cues may include soft footsteps, page turns, sparse laptop taps, sleep breathing, social chirps, and gentle music. Sound remains off by default, is instantly mutable, stops in quiet/fullscreen/suspend, is rate-limited, and never fires on every frame. Unknown cues are ignored. Assets must be original, licensed, or explicitly approved.

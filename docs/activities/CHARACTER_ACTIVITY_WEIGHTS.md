# PokoLoko — Character Activity Weights

## Design principle

Weights describe tendencies, not scripts. Legal posture, interruption safety, cooldowns, session memory, and quiet/fullscreen rules are applied before weighted chance.

## Relative identity

| Activity | Poko | Loko | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Laptop | unsupported | 1.55 | Loko’s focused signature; no Poko asset exists |
| Reading | unsupported | 1.65 | Loko’s strongest calm identity activity |
| Music | 1.35 | 0.88 | Poko is more expressive; Loko is restrained |
| Ball play | 1.55 | 0.52 | Poko signature; Loko rare surprise |
| Peeking | 1.35 | 0.68 | Poko explores more often |
| Drink | 0.85 | 0.95 | Both ambient; Loko more deliberate |
| Eat | 0.95 | 0.86 | Both ambient and infrequent |

These values multiply context and drive fit. They are not final probabilities.

## Duration identity

- Poko music: approximately 5–13 seconds.
- Poko ball play: one authored phrase, approximately 6–12 seconds including choreography.
- Loko laptop: approximately 14–42 seconds.
- Loko reading: approximately 14–40 seconds.
- Loko music: approximately 6–15 seconds.

The controller still exits at safe boundaries, so elapsed duration is a request rather than permission to cut through a prop-bearing frame.

## Frequency protection

- High-salience Poko play has a minimum five-minute gap and at most two starts per hour.
- Loko ball play has a minimum ten-minute gap and at most one start per hour.
- Laptop and reading use multi-minute cooldowns and category spacing.
- Food and drink are occasional ambience and never become care obligations.
- Recent interrupted sessions receive a reselection penalty.

## Activity-level setting

- Calm multiplies activity integration scores by 0.72.
- Balanced uses 1.00.
- Lively uses 1.28.

Quiet/fullscreen/lock suppression takes precedence. Lively mode cannot force a visually or physically invalid routine.

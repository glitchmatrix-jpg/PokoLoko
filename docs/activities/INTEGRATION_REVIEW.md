# Step 19 Integration Review

## Accepted integrations

All 12 Step 18 character-specific definitions remain approved and are now governed by explicit personality, context, duration, and frequency policy. Thirteen activity animations are covered because Loko peeking uses two approved phrases.

## Explicit limitations

- Poko laptop and reading are not integrated because no authoritative assets exist.
- No standalone yawn, stretch, or ear-only routine is promoted from archival poses.
- Poko and Loko food/drink props remain composite frames.
- Peeking requires native screen-edge alignment and will stay unavailable when no safe edge destination exists.
- Audio and typing providers remain optional; their absence reduces context weighting rather than disabling autonomous life.

## Interruption review

- Drag, character switch, display loss, and shutdown use immediate generation invalidation and recovery.
- Pause/fullscreen quieting uses safe phrase boundaries where props are present.
- Reading waits for page-rest or loop-boundary.
- Ball play waits for ball-rest unless an immediate interruption occurs.
- Eating remains forward-only; reverse or ping-pong is prohibited.

## Quarantine decisions

No Step 18 approved activity was newly rejected. Unsupported character/activity pairs remain unavailable rather than being disguised with mirrored, recolored, or crossfaded substitutes.

## Review dimensions

Score each from 1–5 every 30 minutes:

- mechanical repetition;
- annoyance or attention demand;
- Poko/Loko personality distinction;
- contextual appropriateness;
- activity pacing;
- transition continuity;
- prop continuity;
- visual novelty fatigue;
- perceived companionship;
- desire to keep the companion running.

A score of 1 or 2 requires a diagnostic trace and written note.

## Irritation limits

The following are release blockers:

- repeated high-salience action three times consecutively;
- affectionate or annoyed reaction without corresponding interaction;
- crying without an explicit contextual trigger;
- peeking repeatedly at the wrong screen edge;
- frequent context-driven laptop/music behavior that feels deterministic;
- more than one unsolicited high-salience reaction in ten minutes at Calm;
- prop popping or locomotion beginning while a prop remains visible;
- waking and sleeping repeatedly in a short cycle.

# V0.1 Scope — Living Companion Foundation

## Scope philosophy

V0.1 is not a tiny technical demo and does not exile the fun activities. It is the first complete, emotionally coherent product slice: both characters must walk, rest, sleep, respond, and perform signature contextual and spontaneous activities through the same final architecture.

## Required product systems

- authoritative state machine;
- deterministic animation player;
- smooth native locomotion;
- direction and screen-boundary behavior;
- visible-region dragging and settlement;
- hidden mind and session rhythm;
- personality-specific action scoring;
- optional local contextual awareness;
- interruption levels and safe recovery;
- tray, settings, pause, quiet mode, and character switch;
- diagnostic animation/transition laboratory;
- branded onboarding and native identity using the final logo/icon system.

## Required foundational behavior

Both characters must support:

- stable idle with sparse ambient variation;
- left and right walking with real X movement;
- stop/orientation recovery without abrupt flips;
- sleep entry, sleep loop, and validated wake recovery;
- drag interruption and release settlement;
- safe character switching;
- display/work-area/fullscreen restraint.

## Required character activities

### Poko

- music;
- ball play;
- peeking tied to a real edge;
- laptop or focus imitation during typing context;
- eating/drinking;
- ambient blink/look;
- restrained contextual social/emotional reactions.

### Loko

- reading;
- laptop work;
- music;
- rare ball play;
- peeking;
- eating/drinking;
- calm idle;
- restrained love reaction.

Every included activity must satisfy the activity experience contract. Assets requiring unresolved unsafe bridges remain documented but not silently substituted.

## Context included

- typing activity presence/rate only;
- pointer activity intensity;
- system idle;
- time-of-day bucket;
- fullscreen state;
- lock/resume;
- optional audio-playback presence if reliable and permission-light.

No content inspection is included.

## User controls

- character;
- calm/balanced/lively activity level;
- contextual awareness master toggle;
- typing-response toggle;
- audio-presence toggle if implemented;
- sound and volume;
- always-on-top;
- size at safe integer scales;
- pause;
- reduced motion;
- fullscreen/quiet behavior;
- diagnostics in development or explicit advanced settings.

## Explicit exclusions

Excluded because they do not support the initial companion experience or would distort architecture:

- cloud accounts;
- multiplayer;
- AI chat;
- text reading or semantic app awareness;
- inventory and outfits;
- progression currencies;
- care meters;
- achievements designed for retention;
- online analytics as a requirement;
- weather integration;
- notifications unrelated to pet control.

These are exclusions, not a vague future backlog.

## Release gate

V0.1 is not complete until both characters can remain active for multi-hour sessions without obvious repetition, privacy ambiguity, grounding failures, stale transitions, prop popping, or attention pressure.

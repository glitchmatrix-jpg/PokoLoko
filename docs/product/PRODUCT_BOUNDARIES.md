# Product Boundaries

## The product is

- a desktop companion;
- an ambient life-sim;
- a playful local application;
- a character-driven animation and behavior system;
- optional, pausable, and user-controlled.

## The product is not

- a productivity surveillance tool;
- a keylogger;
- a mental-health authority;
- a fake sentient being;
- an obligation simulator;
- a notification engine wearing a cute skin;
- a monetized guilt loop.

## No guilt mechanics

There will be no:

- hunger damage;
- neglect punishment;
- “Poko missed you” pressure after ordinary absence;
- streaks;
- death or illness from inactivity;
- sadness triggered because the user closed or paused the app;
- manipulative reminders to return.

## No invasive surveillance

The app never reads content. Context is local, minimal, optional, short-lived, and disclosed. No cloud upload is required for contextual behavior.

## No fake sentience claims

Product language may be playful but must not claim consciousness, feelings, dependence, or personal understanding. Character mood is an animation/behavior model.

## No exhausting care obligations

Users may leave the pet alone for hours. The pet should self-regulate through rest and activity without requiring feeding, cleaning, or constant reassurance.

## No visual instability for feature count

An activity is excluded from a release until its entry, loop, interruption, exit, grounding, and prop continuity are acceptable. “More animations” is not a quality metric.

## No random emotional harm

Crying or sadness assets are used only in gentle, contextually justified, non-manipulative ways. They are never random background entertainment and never blame the user.

## No hidden platform behavior

Startup, always-on-top, contextual awareness, sound, fullscreen behavior, and diagnostics are explicit settings.

## No irreversible personality profiling

Session memory is ephemeral. The app does not infer or store work habits, emotional state, identity, or long-term preferences from desktop activity.

## Product restraint

PokoLoko should sometimes do nothing. Presence is not measured by constant motion, sound, reaction, or interruption.
